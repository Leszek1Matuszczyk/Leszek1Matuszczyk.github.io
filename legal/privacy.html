Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

LOGIKON is a logic-puzzle game published by ElEm Software. Progress is stored on your device. Ads are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

What data is processed

Game progress (solved puzzles, stars, points, day streak, sound and ads settings) is stored only locally on your device — in the browser’s localStorage or in the app’s local storage. ElEm Software does not operate a game server that receives this data.

Google Play Data safety

LOGIKON does not collect, share, or sell personal data. Optional first-party house ads do not send advertising identifiers. If a live ad network is later enabled in a store update, this policy and the Play Data safety form will be updated first.

Ads

After you allow ads, LOGIKON may show a banner on menu screens, a short full-screen ad after every few solved puzzles, and an optional rewarded ad that unlocks a hint. Until publisher IDs are configured the game serves first-party house ads that work offline. If AdMob or AdSense IDs are later filled in, those networks may process device identifiers according to their own policies. You can refuse ads on first launch or disable them in Settings.

What we do not do

  • we do not create accounts,
  • we do not require an email address or a login,
  • we do not sell data,
  • we do not use ads when you have declined them.

Permissions

The app does not need access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 within the meaning of COPPA and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

You can reset progress in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes local data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the email address listed on the app’s Google Play listing.
