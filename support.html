Support

LOGIKON by ElEm Software

Turn ads off

Open Settings in the app and switch Ads off. You can also refuse ads the first time the consent sheet appears. Hints still cost an in-game hint charge; turning ads off does not unlock free hints.

Reset progress

Settings → Reset progress. This clears solved puzzles, stars, points and the day streak on this device. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

Timer and points

Easy 90 seconds (max 100 points), Standard 60 seconds (180), Hard 30 seconds (300). Points scale with time left. A hint multiplies the score by 0.6. A wrong answer subtracts 12. Timeout scores 0 and does not count as solved.

Contact

Use the support email on the Google Play listing for the developer account that publishes LOGIKON (ElEm Software). Please include the app version (1.0) and package name com.elemsoftware.logikon.
